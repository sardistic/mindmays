Three ways into the archive

Choose your game

Entries turns encyclopedia knowledge into three different browser games. Every mode is free and requires no account.

Classic

A fixed-view, 1990s-style adventure through 100 painted chambers. Open doors with multiple-choice knowledge seals, choose subjects and difficulty, and keep a persistent score card.

Play Classic

Walkable 3D

Move freely through a first-person knowledge maze. Invite friends into the same named keep and watch one another explore and open passages in real time.

Enter the 3D keep

Sudden Death

Ten typed-answer questions become steadily harder. There are no multiple-choice options and no second life: one mistake closes the run.

Attempt Sudden Death

Not sure where to start?

Classic is the fullest introduction to Entries. It teaches the rules gradually, lets you research every answer, and saves your progress in this browser. Sudden Death is best for a quick challenge; Walkable 3D is best with company.
