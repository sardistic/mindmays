Behind the archive

Built for the pleasure of finding things out

Entries is an original browser knowledge game in the spirit of exploratory multimedia encyclopedias: painted rooms, curious inhabitants, obscure doors and a real article waiting behind every question.

Why Entries exists

Modern search is efficient, but efficiency is not the same as discovery. Entries turns reference material back into a place to wander. The game rewards correct answers, but it also makes room for following a link, reading a source and leaving with a fact you did not expect to learn.

The Unwritten Keep

The setting is a 100-room archive rendered in the visual language of 1990s CD-ROM adventures. Its rooms, inhabitants, interface, questions and procedural sound are original to the project. No Encarta names, artwork, maps, text or other protected material are used.

Wikipedia and attribution

Wikipedia supplies reference material and destination articles under its applicable licenses. Entries is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wikipedia or the Wikimedia Foundation. Knowledge credits belong to Wikipedia and its contributors.

Who made it

Entries is published by Sardistic Multimedia. It is designed as a small web game that can be played immediately, without registration, installation or a storefront.
