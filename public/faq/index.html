Entries FAQ

The short answers about playing, researching and exploring together.

Is Entries free?

Yes. Entries runs in a modern web browser, requires no account, and has no paid gameplay.

Is Entries an official Wikipedia game?

No. Entries is an independent game. It uses and links to knowledge from Wikipedia and credits its contributors, but it is not produced or endorsed by the Wikimedia Foundation.

Where do the questions come from?

The question bank is authored for Entries and each factual prompt cites a relevant Wikipedia article. Automated checks confirm that sources still resolve and that supported answers remain present in the referenced material.

How does multiplayer work?

Choose the same shared keep name as another player. In supported modes, players in that keep can see one another and share opened doors or passages. No account is required.

Does the game save progress?

Classic progress and preferences are stored locally in your browser. Clearing site data, switching browsers, or moving to another device does not carry that save with you.

Can I use Wikipedia while answering?

Yes. Research is an intentional game mechanic. A lower score after research represents the cost of opening the archive, not a rule against learning.

Which mode should I try first?

Start with Classic for the complete adventure. Choose Sudden Death for a short typed-answer challenge or Walkable 3D for first-person multiplayer exploration.
