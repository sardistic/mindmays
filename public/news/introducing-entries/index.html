Introducing Entries

Entries is now open: a free browser game where encyclopedia questions unlock the doors of a strange, hand-painted archive.

A place to wander through knowledge

The web is very good at giving us the thing we asked for. Entries is interested in the thing we did not know to ask. Its Unwritten Keep contains 100 chambers, a large question bank, peculiar inhabitants and direct paths into Wikipedia whenever a subject deserves more than a guess.

You can play the fixed-view Classic adventure, walk the keep in first person with friends, or attempt a ten-question Sudden Death run. Research is allowed in every corner where it appears. The point is not merely to prove what you know; it is to leave knowing more.

Entries requires no account and runs directly in the browser. Open the first seal, or read the player's guide before entering.
