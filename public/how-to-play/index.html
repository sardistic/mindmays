How to play Entries

Entries is a free Wikipedia-powered knowledge game set inside the Unwritten Keep. Choose a door, answer the knowledge seal behind it, and turn every correct answer into a new path through the archive.

Start a new questChoose a mode

The basic loop

  1. Enter a chamber and choose one of its painted doors.
  2. Answer the Wikipedia-sourced question on the knowledge seal.
  3. A correct answer opens that door permanently and adds lore to your score.
  4. Explore the next room, examine its objects, and keep charting the 100-room castle.

Every question cites a Wikipedia article. Opening the research drawer is part of the game, not cheating: Entries is built to reward curiosity as much as recall.

Matches, mistakes, and recovery

You begin Classic with five matches. A wrong answer extinguishes one and replaces the question with an easier seal. Every fourth opened seal relights a match, so a careful run can recover. Losing the fifth match ends the current quest, while your best score remains on the score card.

You may also burn a match voluntarily to reveal the route you have already walked.

Controls

Classic

  • Click a painted door or use the left and right arrow keys to move.
  • Examine a room to open its encyclopedia article.
  • Speak with inhabitants; they remember repeated questions.
  • Choose one of twelve subject areas and four difficulty levels.

Walkable 3D

  • Use W and S to walk, A and D to turn, and E to interact.
  • On touch screens, use the on-screen movement pad.
  • Share the same keep name with friends to explore together.

Sudden Death

  • Type each answer and submit it before moving on.
  • There are ten questions and one life. One wrong answer ends the run.
